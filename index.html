TV Lounge is a player for user-provided playlists. It does not sell, bundle, or provide television channels, movies, series, or subscriptions.

Read the privacy policy Get support
01

Player only

You add your own compatible playlist or service connection. TV Lounge does not provide media content.

02

Stored on your TV

Playlist details, settings, favourites, watch history, and playback positions are stored locally on the television.

03

Direct connections

Your TV connects directly to the playlist and streaming servers you choose. TV Lounge has no intermediary content server.

Support

Need help?

For a useful report, include the Samsung TV model year, the TV Lounge version, the action you performed, and the exact error message. Never publish playlist URLs, usernames, passwords, or access tokens.

Open GitHub support

Use the public issue tracker for support. Never post playlist credentials, private URLs, or other sensitive information.

Kurzfassung auf Deutsch

TV Lounge ist ausschließlich ein Player.

Die App liefert keine Sender, Filme, Serien, Abonnements oder Playlists. Vom Nutzer eingetragene Playlist-Daten, Einstellungen, Favoriten und Wiedergabestände werden lokal auf dem Fernseher gespeichert. Der Fernseher verbindet sich direkt mit den vom Nutzer ausgewählten Servern.
